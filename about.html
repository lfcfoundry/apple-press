Apple Press

What it is and how it works

Apple Press is a free clicker game for kids in a single web page. Tap the apple to pick apples, buy upgrades, and move through stages that each bring a promotion party, a new animated scene and new upgrades. The whole game, with scenes, fonts and code, is one HTML file. It runs with no server and keeps progress in your browser, so after one load it needs no internet.

Why it exists

A gentle clicker for children and the grown-ups who sit with them: free, with no ads, purchases or accounts, no sounds, no timers or streaks, and nothing to trade. It also tests how much game fits in one small file anyone can save, send to a friend and open offline.

Built for kids

Big tap targets, no timers or streaks, and a Grown-ups door in front of links.

13 stages

A first adventure of six stages, then a longer journey, with a sticker book of 49 stickers.

No sound

A visual and tactile game, with less stimulation for sensory challenges. Calm effects tone down motion.

Send it to a friend

One file, or two smaller copies for messages and email. Double-click to open.

How it plays

Each press earns apples. Upgrades add apples per press and per second. Own 10, 25 or 50 of the same upgrade and every copy gets twice as strong. Each copy of an upgrade costs 17% more than the last, so there is always a next thing to save for. Helpers keep picking at half speed while the game is closed, for up to 8 hours.

Features for grown-ups

No sound, by design

The game has no sound or music at all. It is a visual and tactile experience: the apple squishes under a finger, things sparkle and move, and nothing beeps or startles. That means less stimulation for players with sensory challenges, and no volume to manage.

Safe saving

Progress autosaves in your browser and keeps a backup copy. Save a code or a file to move to another device, and load it again anywhere.

A Grown-ups door

Start over and the links to other games ask for a 3-second hold and a drag first. A small child is unlikely to pass; it is a speed bump, not a lock.

Effects that adapt

Full, Lite and Calm effects. The game steps down by itself on a slow device, and offers Calm if your device asks for reduced motion. It never switches on its own.

A quick tour

New players get a short guided look at the page in simple words. It can be skipped, and opened again from the How to play button.

Hold to press

Hold the apple down and it keeps pressing, which helps tired hands and younger players. It can be switched off in the footer.

Help when something is slow

Tap five times in the top-left corner to save a speed report or an error log as a small file. Nothing is sent anywhere.
