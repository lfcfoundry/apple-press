# Apple Press playtest card

One page for a grown-up sitting with a child (about 6 and up) who is trying the game. Takes about 15 minutes. You do not need to know anything about the game.

**Before you start**
- Open https://lfcfoundry.github.io/apple-press/ on the device the child will use. Note the device and browser, and the version number at the very bottom of the page.
- For a first-time test use a browser or device where the game has never been opened (a private window works). Do not show the child anything first.

**While they play (watch, do not help)**
1. Say only: "Here is a game. Try it, and tell me what you are thinking." Then stay quiet for five minutes.
2. If they ask what to do, say "What do you think?" Count how often they ask.
3. Write down, with the time:
   - the first thing they touch, and how long before they tap the big apple;
   - any spot where they stop and look puzzled for more than ten seconds;
   - anything they tap that does nothing;
   - anything they say out loud, in their own words (a quote is worth more than your summary).
4. After about five minutes, if they have not found the upgrades, do not point. Note that they had not found them.

**Afterward, ask three questions**
- "What was the best part?"
- "Was anything confusing or boring?"
- "What would you change?"

**Note for each question: a short answer in their words.** Then ask a grown-up question for yourself: did the game feel calm, or pushy? (It has no timers, ads or purchases, so nothing should feel pushy.)

**Safety check (30 seconds)**
- Did the child tap anything that took them out of the game? (It should stay on the page.)
- Did they get stuck anywhere they could not get out of without help?

**Send back**
Copy these notes into a message with the device, the browser, the version number and the child's age. A photo of the screen where they got stuck helps most. Names and faces are not needed and should not be shared.

Thank you for helping.
